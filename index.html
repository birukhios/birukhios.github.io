Brand identity — Sorenie Import & Export

Final proposal, for LifeWire

A full identity for an Ethiopian import-and-export company: a guinea fowl drawn into the letterform, tangerine orange against sage green, a generative feather pattern, and the applications the mark actually has to survive — signage, packaging, vehicles, print.

Designing at the intersection of strategy, craft and usability.

I am a Product Manager and Designer based in Addis Ababa with 7+ years of experience leading products from concept to launch. My work blends strategic vision, roadmap planning, stakeholder management and user-centred design across HR and ERP platforms, immersive 3D products, ecommerce, education and consulting.

A degree in Architecture and Urban Planning gave me a spatial design foundation that still shapes how I see systems, structure and visual hierarchy. Product work is the same discipline at a different scale.

My love language is a meaningful conversation. Outside work: sketching, vibecoding, movies, games and research.
